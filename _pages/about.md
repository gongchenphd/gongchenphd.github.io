I am a physician scientist trained at **Heidelberg University Medical Faculty, Germany**.

I obtained my PhD in **General, Visceral and Transplantation Surgery** at Heidelberg University. Previously, I completed doctoral training in **Biomedical Sciences (Medicine)** at **KU Leuven, Belgium**.

My research focuses on **translational oncology, tumor cell plasticity, and molecular mechanisms underlying cancer progression**.


## Research Interests

My current research interests include:

- Tumor cell plasticity and phenotypic reprogramming
- Pancreatic cancer biology
- Gastric cancer heterogeneity
- Single-cell transcriptomics and spatial transcriptomics
- Cancer metabolism and precision oncology


## Research Experience

### Pancreatic Cancer Research

My doctoral research investigated osteogenic-like phenotypic reprogramming in pancreatic cancer cells and its influence on malignant behaviors through MAPK–ERK signaling.

### Gastric Cancer Research

I applied single-cell transcriptomic analysis to identify cancer cell states, metabolic signatures, and potential therapeutic targets in gastric cancer.
